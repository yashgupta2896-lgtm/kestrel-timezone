Check meetings already on the books

New bookings are safe by design. But meetings created the old way can still be wrong. Kestrel audits each upcoming meeting and flags anything that will drift on a daylight-saving change or was saved without a timezone — so you can re-anchor it before a customer shows up at the wrong time.
